Connecticut Know-How

Connecticut Taxes We Handle

Connecticut has rules of its own. These come up often for our clients:

  • CT-1040 resident income tax returns
  • CT-1040NR/PY for part-year residents and people who moved in or out of state
  • CT-1040X amended Connecticut returns
  • CT-1120 corporation business tax returns
  • Connecticut's elective pass-through entity tax for S-Corps and partnerships — and whether electing it helps you
  • Multi-state returns for Connecticut businesses with customers or owners in other states

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you only work with Connecticut clients?
No. We're based in Hartford County, but we work with clients in all 50 states and prepare returns for whichever states you need to file in.
I moved into or out of Connecticut this year. What do I file?
Part-year residents generally file Form CT-1040NR/PY, which splits your income between the time you lived in Connecticut and the time you didn't. You may also need a return in the other state.
Should my business elect Connecticut's pass-through entity tax?
Connecticut's pass-through entity tax is elective. For some S-Corp and partnership owners, paying state tax at the business level produces federal tax savings; for others it doesn't. We can look at the numbers for your business.
Can we meet in person?
Most of our clients work with us remotely — by phone, video and secure document upload — which is often the fastest way to get your return done.
What does it cost?
It depends on the type of return and how many years are involved. Reach out for a free consultation and we'll give you a quote.
