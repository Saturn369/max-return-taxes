Privacy Policy

What we collect through this website, how we use it, and your choices.

This policy explains what information Max Return Taxes collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions? Email freedomenka@gmail.com or call (860) 993-9904.

Information you give us

When you use a contact form on this site, we collect what you enter: your name, email address, phone number, state, the service you're interested in and your message. We also receive whatever you share with us directly by email or phone.

If you become a client, we collect the tax documents and information needed to prepare your returns. That information is handled separately from this website.

Please don't send Social Security numbers, tax documents or bank details through the website form. Once we're working together, we'll give you a secure way to share them.

How contact form submissions are delivered

Our contact forms are processed by Web3Forms, a form delivery service that sends your message to our email inbox. Web3Forms handles the data only to deliver it to us.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and give you a quote
  • To provide tax preparation and related services if you become a client
  • To meet our legal and professional obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Tax return information

As tax return preparers, we're subject to federal rules that restrict how we use and disclose tax return information. We don't use or disclose your tax return information for any purpose other than preparing your returns without your consent, except as the law permits or requires.

Website analytics

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count page views and clicks on our phone number, email and contact form. GoatCounter doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across other websites, and doesn't identify individual visitors. We see only totals, like how many people viewed a page, which site they came from, and their general browser, device type and country.

Other services this site uses

  • Hosting: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts: our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers. See Google's privacy policy.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiries for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and client records for as long as required by law and professional standards.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your inquiry, subject to any records we're legally required to keep. Just email freedomenka@gmail.com.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you give us. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, which is why we ask you not to send sensitive tax documents through the website form.

Children

This website isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information through it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date below.
